About Us
Our Story: Taste the Tradition

From the Land of Sri Krishna to the Heart of Tamil Nadu

Every great tradition has a journey, and ours begins more than four decades ago. Forty-four years ago, a master kulfi artisan traveled from the sacred soil of Mathura and Brindavan—the land of Lord Krishna—bringing with him an authentic, time-honored craft and a secret legacy of traditional kulfi-making. He planted these deep roots in Ghatkopar, Mumbai, where this slow-cooked, pure-milk art was perfected and cherished by generations of dessert lovers.
Among those dedicated to preserving this craft was our master chef- Harvendra Gurjar, who spent over 20 years mastering every nuance of traditional kulfi-making. He learned that true kulfi cannot be rushed—it requires patience, pure milk, an uncompromising dedication to natural ingredients and most importantly- LOVE.

A New Chapter in Coimbatore

Today, that rich 44-year lineage finds a vibrant new home here in Kannappa Nagar, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Led by our master chef, Sri Shyam Kulfi & Ice Cream brings you the authentic, nostalgic taste of slow-churned heritage, completely free from artificial preservatives.
While we hold sacred the traditional recipes handed down through decades, we also love to delight modern palates. Alongside our classic kulfi sticks and rich slices, we introduce exciting, novel innovations—including our special sugar-free options for the health-conscious.
Pure milk. Traditional love. Playful new flavours. We invite you and your family to share in a sweet tradition that bridges generations, cities, and hearts.
